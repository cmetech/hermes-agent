# Safety and interpretation

Laptop evidence is sanitized and fictional; it never describes the real machine. Resilience uses harmless bounded waits and fail-once state only. A timeout scenario should retain a failed workflow lifecycle while its typed-timeout and process-cleanup claims can pass. AI may cost money and needs explicit digest-bound consent. Scheduling creates a unique `repeat=1` Hermes cron job only with explicit consent. Destructive, corruption, exhaustion, flood, and soak modes are not user-addressable showcases.
