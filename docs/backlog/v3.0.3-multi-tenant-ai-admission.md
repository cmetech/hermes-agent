# v3.0.3 follow-up: multi-tenant or remote AI admission

Status: conditional, deferred, and non-normative for Workflow v3.0.3 Revision 7.

Revision 7 intentionally has no consent endpoint, token, dialog, HMAC
machinery, or caller-supplied capability. Its AI admission assumes the current
local operator deployment model and server-owned runner/runtime authority.

If deployment becomes multi-tenant or permits remote operators, AI admission
must receive a new threat model before exposure. That work must define
principal and tenant boundaries, authorization ownership, replay resistance,
auditability, revocation, scheduling composition, and prompt/input isolation.
It must not retrofit consent theater onto the local Revision 7 contract.
