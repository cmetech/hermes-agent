# v3.0.3 follow-up: USD budget ledger

Status: deferred and non-normative for Workflow v3.0.3 Revision 7.

Revision 7 enforces existing execution, timeout, retry, worker, subprocess, and
process-tree limits. It does not introduce a currency-denominated budget.

A future USD ledger needs provider price provenance, model/version identity,
unknown-price behavior, reservation and reconciliation semantics, retries and
subagents, concurrency safety, and a fail-closed policy that does not mistake
token or time limits for money. It must not add a caller-supplied entitlement
or a behavioral environment variable.
